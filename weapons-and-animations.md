# Robot Fighter v2 — Weapons & Attack Animations

Robot-themed weapon set for the game. All weapons follow the v2 visual rules: kid-friendly, colorful, clean, subtly aggressive, angular/faceted style, dark glowing visor on the wielder. Each entry lists the animation beats (windup → active → recovery) so the motion reads crispy in-game: fast startup, snappy active frames, clear recovery.

## Batch 1 — animated (videos in `weapon-anims/`)

### 1. Volt Gauntlets
- **Type:** Rushdown / close-range. Oversized forearm gauntlets with exposed electric coils.
- **Animation — "Surge Combo":** crouch windup, coils spark → three rapid punches (jab, cross, hook), each trailing lightning arcs → finisher uppercut with a vertical lightning burst → quick shake-off recovery.
- **Game feel:** fastest startup of the set; hit-stop on the uppercut.

### 2. Plasma Saber
- **Type:** Balanced / mid-range. Hilt on the hip ignites into a glowing energy blade.
- **Animation — "Helix Slash":** draw + ignite (blade extends with a hum flash) → 360° spinning slash, blade leaving a light trail → reverse-grip downward chop → twirl to extinguish.
- **Game feel:** the spin covers both sides; trail effect sells the speed.

### 3. Railgun Arm Cannon
- **Type:** Zoner / long-range. Forearm-mounted cannon with charging coils.
- **Animation — "Coil Breaker":** raise arm, coils glow brighter in 3 stages (audio/visual charge tell) → fire: bright blue bolt, heavy recoil pushes the mech back a step → vent steam from the barrel during recovery.
- **Game feel:** the staged charge is the dodge tell — fair and readable.

### 4. Mag Chakram
- **Type:** Tricky / mid-range. Spinning magnetic disc with a glowing rim.
- **Animation — "Orbit Toss":** sidearm windup, disc spins up → throw: disc flies out in a wide loop, rim glowing → boomerang return, mech catches it cleanly → reset stance.
- **Game feel:** hits on the way out AND the way back; catch timing is the skill check.

### 5. Seismic Hammer
- **Type:** Heavy / grappler-friendly. Big piston-driven hammer, hydraulic hiss on the swing.
- **Animation — "Fault Line":** slow heavy lift overhead (the tell) → slam down, piston fires → circular shockwave ring expands along the ground → hammer rests, mech yanks it free.
- **Game feel:** slowest startup, biggest payoff; shockwave ring is the area threat.

### 6. Cryo Sprayer
- **Type:** Control / close-mid. Arm nozzle that sprays freezing mist.
- **Animation — "Deep Freeze":** nozzle flips up, frost forms on the tip → sweeping spray in a cone, ice crystals frosting the ground → shake off ice buildup on the arm.
- **Game feel:** applies a slow/frost effect; the frosted ground is the visual zone marker.

## Batch 2 — queued (not yet animated)

### 7. Arc Whip
- **Type:** Pokes / mid-range. Coiled cable whip crackling with energy.
- **Animation — "Live Wire":** crack the whip overhead → long lash outward with an electric snap → recoil coil back to the arm.
- **Game feel:** longest reach; the crack is the timing cue.

### 8. Aegis Pulse Shield
- **Type:** Defensive / counter. Hex-pattern energy shield on the forearm.
- **Animation — "Rebound":** shield flares up (hex grid flashes) → absorb the hit, shield ripples → counter-bash: shield slam forward with a pulse shockwave.
- **Game feel:** the defensive option; successful block fuels the counter.

## Batch 3 — 10 more (all animated)

### 9. Tesla Coil Backpack
- **Type:** Area denial. Backpack with twin Tesla coils.
- **Animation — "Storm Cell":** coils spin up, arcs jumping between them → crouch and discharge: lightning nova explodes outward in all directions → coils vent smoke.
- **Game feel:** the "get off me" button; hits everything nearby.

### 10. Drill Fist
- **Type:** Rushdown / armor-break. Forearm that splits open into a spinning drill.
- **Animation — "Bore Through":** fist clenches, drill bit spins up with rising whine → dashing drill punch straight through → drill retracts with a hydraulic clunk.
- **Game feel:** eats through blocks; the whine is the audio tell.

### 11. Grapple Harpoon
- **Type:** Grappler / setup. Shoulder-mounted harpoon gun with a tether cable.
- **Animation — "Reel In":** aim with a laser sight line → fire: harpoon shoots out trailing cable → on hit, yank the cable: enemy gets dragged in → follow-up point-blank blast.
- **Game feel:** the come-here tool; whiffed harpoon is very punishable.

### 12. Rocket Fists
- **Type:** Poke / mid-range. Fists that detach and rocket forward.
- **Animation — "Double Impact":** fists pop off with thruster flames → both fists rocket forward in parallel → retract on cables, snap back into the wrists.
- **Game feel:** two-hit poke at surprising range; the cable snap-back is the recovery.

### 13. Grav Slam
- **Type:** Heavy / area. Gravity-core boots and gauntlets.
- **Animation — "Event Horizon":** leap high, body glowing violet → meteor slam down, gravity pulse ring crushes outward → dust and debris settle.
- **Game feel:** the anti-air punish; huge landing, huge recovery.

### 14. EMP Orb
- **Type:** Zoner / debuff. Palm-sized orb crackling with static.
- **Animation — "Blackout":** overhand throw, orb floats forward slowly, sparking → detonate: blue-white EMP flash dome → enemies caught inside short-circuit and stagger.
- **Game feel:** slow projectile, big payoff; the flash dome is the visual zone.

### 15. Plasma Mortar
- **Type:** Zoner / long-range. Back-mounted mortar tube.
- **Animation — "Fireworks":** tube angles up, plasma glob charges → lob: arcing glob explodes on landing in a splash.
- **Game feel:** the arc goes over obstacles; landing marker keeps it fair.

### 16. Sonic Cannon
- **Type:** Control / mid-range. Chest-mounted speaker dish.
- **Animation — "Feedback":** dish unfolds, rings pulse → blast: visible concentric sound rings knock back.
- **Game feel:** the pushback tool; resets spacing.

### 17. Nanite Swarm
- **Type:** Tricky / setplay. Hive pod on the shoulder.
- **Animation — "Hive Mind":** pod opens, tiny drones pour out and orbit → command gesture: swarm dives at the enemy in a spiral.
- **Game feel:** delayed multi-hit; the orbit time is the counterplay window.

### 18. Prism Beam
- **Type:** Zoner / precision. Forearm prism emitter.
- **Animation — "Split Decision":** prism unfolds, light gathers → fire: single beam splits into three mid-flight.
- **Game feel:** hard to dodge clean; the split point is the mind game.
